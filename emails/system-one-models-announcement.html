System One Models: What Jev and Laya can do that Claude can't

Until now we've been using huge reasoning models to label data and score things, which is like renting a crane to hang a picture up on your wall. Then TypeSafe AI launched Jev, a model that can't write a single sentence but answers typed questions in a fraction of a second, and three days later a startup in India released a free, open-weights version called Laya.

I wrote about how these System One models differ from LLMs and what people have already built with them, including a Chrome extension of mine that uses Laya to sort the articles I save.
