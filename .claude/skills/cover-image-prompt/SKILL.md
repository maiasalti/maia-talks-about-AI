# Cover image prompt

Maia generates every post cover herself in Nano Banana (Gemini). Your job is to write the prompt,
not the image, and to make sure the result matches the covers already on the site.

## 1. Re-anchor on the existing covers

Before writing anything, look at 3 or 4 of the current covers (the `image:` frontmatter field of each
post in `src/posts/*.mdx` points to them under `public/`). The house style drifts if you work from
memory. As of September 2026 they share:

- **Line and fill:** flat cartoon editorial illustration, bold even-weight dark charcoal outlines,
  flat colour fills with very soft cel shading. No gradients-heavy 3D, no photorealism.
- **Palette:** muted sage green, teal, mustard/ochre yellow, warm cream, powder-blue sky or wall.
  One warm accent (orange/coral or mustard) at most.
- **Decoration:** a few small doodles in the empty space: four-point sparkles, plus signs, hollow
  circles (sometimes music notes or triangles). Same outline style as the main subject.
- **Composition:** one central symbolic object or scene, calm and uncluttered, wide landscape frame
  (roughly 16:9 to 2:1), readable as a small thumbnail on the blog card.
- **Recurring props (optional):** retro beige computer, floppy disks, wooden desk, GPUs, rockets.

Reference covers: `public/images/j-space/cover.png`, `public/images/idiot-index-of-tokens/cover.png`,
`public/images/attention-is-still-all-you-need/cover.png`, `public/images/blog-1/cover.png`,
`public/images/deepseek-scaling-law/cover.png`.

## 2. Pick the concept

Read the post, then find **one** visual metaphor for its core idea. Good covers are symbolic, not
literal screenshots of the topic: reading a model's mind through glasses (J-space), endless GPUs
(scaling law), a logic gate sorting inputs into probability trays (System One models). If the post
has a running joke or analogy (a heading, a TL;DR, a metaphor), prefer a concept that echoes it.

If Maia hasn't picked a direction, offer 4 or 5 concepts with a one-line reason each and recommend
one. When she picks, write the full prompt.

## 3. Write the prompt

Use this skeleton and fill the middle with the concept:

> Wide 16:9 flat cartoon editorial illustration in a clean hand-drawn style: bold, even-weight dark
> charcoal outlines, flat colour fills with very soft shading, muted palette of sage green, teal,
> mustard yellow, warm cream and powder blue. **[Central subject, described as physical objects with
> positions: left, centre, right. One clear focal point.]** **[Background: usually pale powder blue or
> cream, optionally with a faint relevant pattern.]** A few small decorative doodles in the empty
> space: four-point sparkles, little plus signs and hollow circles, in the same outline style. Calm,
> uncluttered composition with plenty of breathing room. No text, no letters, no numbers and no logos
> anywhere in the image.

Rules:

- **Always end with the no-text line.** Nano Banana garbles words (the J-space cover has "systeem",
  "semsoee"). Show quantities with bar meters, sizes or glows instead of numbers.
- **Never name a thing whose identity is text.** An "AND-gate symbol" came back with "AND" written
  on it, even with the no-text line. Avoid logic gates, signs, labelled jars, charts with axes,
  keyboards in close-up and anything described as "labelled". Show categories with colour, shape and
  position instead (e.g. mixed marbles sorted into matching coloured jars), and say the objects are
  "completely blank".
- **No real people's faces unless the post is about them** (the Musk cover is the exception, not the
  pattern), and no company logos, since those also come out garbled.
- Describe objects concretely; avoid abstract words like "intelligence" or "innovation" that the model
  can't draw.
- Keep it to one paragraph. Explain the symbolism to Maia separately, outside the prompt.

## 4. Wire it in

- Save path: `public/images/<post-slug>/cover.png`.
- Frontmatter: `image: "/images/<post-slug>/cover.png"`.
- In the post body, right after the byline:

  ```mdx
  ![<short alt text describing the image>](/images/<post-slug>/cover.png)
  *Generated by Nano Banana*
  ```

Check the post already has these lines before adding them; most drafts include them with the cover
missing, in which case only the image file is needed.
